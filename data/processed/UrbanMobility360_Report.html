📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes NYC Yellow Taxi trip data to identify where, when, and why travel demand, congestion, and inefficient transport usage occur. The analysis produces an interactive Tableau dashboard with a unique Mobility Priority Score that identifies routes and areas needing optimization.

⭐ MOBILITY PRIORITY SCORE (Unique Feature)

Formula:
Priority = (0.30 x Demand + 0.30 x Congestion + 0.20 x Speed_Inv + 0.20 x Duration) x 100

Logic: High Demand + High Congestion + Low Speed + Long Duration = HIGH PRIORITY

💡 Phase 6: Key Insights

📍 WHERE? Mobility Hotspots
Midtown Center, Upper East Side South, Penn Station are top hotspots. Brooklyn's Brownsville has highest per-zone priority (7.6 mph avg speed).
⏰ WHEN? Peak Demand
Busiest hours: 6 PM, 5 PM, 7 PM. Weekday ~75,191 trips/day. Weekend ~76,118 trips/day.
🤔 WHY? Congestion Drivers
Speed drops 4.6% during peak (9.4 vs 9.9 mph). Congestion surges to 64.5% during peak hours.
🚩 WHICH? Inefficient Routes
Midtown Center has 6 high-priority hours. At 5-6 PM, ~10,000 trips at only ~7 mph.
🎯 PRIORITIZE? Recommendations
  • Midtown Center: Dedicated lanes 4-8 PM. Signal optimization. Increase service frequency.
  • Upper East Side South: Transit signal priority for afternoon hours.
  • Brownsville: Infrastructure improvement to raise base speed above 10 mph.
⭐ KEY TAKEAWAY: Midtown Center at 5-7 PM is the #1 mobility problem. 10,890 trips at 7.8 mph, priority score 64.7/100. This zone-hour should be the top priority for transport optimization.
